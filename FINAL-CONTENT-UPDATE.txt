LIMITATIONS / EXISTING DEMO BEHAVIOUR
The supplied FAQ describes the cafe's planned service. The existing prototype
has no selectable product modifiers or real reward redemption. It awards a
demo stamp per completed drink-containing order and shows a reward at seven
stamps. The provided FAQ says the seventh eligible drink is free after six.
The FAQ includes a translated explanation of this difference; the requested
existing loyalty and ordering logic was not changed.
Accounts and saved history remain browser-local. Payments/orders are simulated.
The chatbot remains the existing multilingual FAQ helper unless a live endpoint
is configured. Real testimonials and a WhatsApp Community invite still require
configuration; support WhatsApp links are preserved. No external transaction,
message or production account was created. No live AI backend was tested.
Browser QA used Chromium and responsive iframes, not physical devices or a full
Safari/Firefox matrix. GitHub Pages paths are compatible, but this ZIP was not
published to the live GitHub repository, so its deployed version was not tested.
